# Overview of PTIT and Faculty of Electronic Engineering 1 (FEE1)

## Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT)
- **Institution Name**: Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (Học viện Công nghệ Bưu chính Viễn thông - PTIT).
- **Hanoi Main Campus**: 96A Tran Phu Street, Ha Dong District, Hanoi, Vietnam.
- **Profile**: A premier national university in Vietnam specializing in Information and Communications Technology (ICT), Electronics, Robotics, IoT, and Semiconductor Engineering.

## Faculty of Electronic Engineering 1 (FEE1)
- **Faculty Name**: Faculty of Electronic Engineering 1 (Khoa Kỹ thuật Điện tử 1 - FEE1).
- **Main Office**: Room 205-A2, 2nd Floor, Building A2, Ha Dong Campus, Hanoi.
- **Website**: https://fee1.ptit.edu.vn/
- **Official Email**: fee1@ptit.edu.vn

## Academic Departments
1. **Department of Control and Automation Engineering**: Leads education and research in robotics, industrial automation, autonomous mobile robots (AMR), and intelligent control. Dr. Hoang Son is a senior faculty member in this department.
2. **Department of Electronic Engineering**: Specializes in embedded systems, electronic circuits, and IoT technologies.
3. **Department of Semiconductor Technology**: Focuses on IC design, semiconductor physical layout, and microelectronics.
4. **Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering**: Covers digital systems, computer architecture, and hardware acceleration.

## Research Laboratories
- **OpenWorkShop LAB**: Research and development center for autonomous mobile robots, including the Souta service robot project.
- **Rang Dong Smart Lighting & IoT Lab**: Room 402, Building A3 (joint laboratory with Rang Dong).
- **Panasonic Air Conditioning & Smart Systems Lab**: Room 303, Building A3.
