# Autonomous Reception and Guide Robot Souta

## Robot Identity and Name Meaning
- Robot Name: **Souta** (Kanji: **颯太**).
- Meaning of Name: The name "Souta" in Japanese means **"vast sky"** (or wide open sky). It symbolizes broad perspective, friendliness, and readiness to warmly assist visitors across the institute.

## Developers and Organization
- Research Lab: **OpenWorkShop LAB**.
- Affiliation: **Faculty of Electronic Engineering 1 (FEE1)**, **Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT)**, Hanoi, Vietnam.
- Project Advisor & Lead: **Dr. Hoang Son (TS. Hoàng Sơn)**, senior lecturer at the Department of Control and Automation Engineering.
- Objective: Autonomous lobby service, visitor escorting, and intelligent conversational assistance.

## Technical Specifications
- **Locomotion Platform**: Differential drive robotic base.
- **Microcontroller**: ESP32 connected to ROS 2 via serial link.
- **Software Stack**: ROS 2 Humble (Robot Operating System 2).
- **Navigation & SLAM**: Nav2 stack with 2D LiDAR SLAM and wheel odometry.
- **Sensors**: 2D LiDAR, RGB-D Camera (Intel RealSense), 9-DOF IMU, high-resolution wheel encoders.
- **Human-Robot Interaction**: Touchscreen Kiosk interface (PyQt5) and multi-language AI voice assistant.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Autonomous Destination Guidance**: Escorting guests to designated rooms, laboratories, and faculty offices at PTIT.
2. **Hand Wave & Person Detection**: Detects waving gestures via RGB-D camera and approaches the person safely to initiate service.
3. **Collision Avoidance**: Real-time dynamic obstacle detection and smooth path replanning.
4. **Multilingual AI Voice Chat**: Converses naturally in 5 languages (Vietnamese, English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean).
